We write the answer,
then AI quotes the page.

AI does not quote a sales page. It lifts a short, clean answer to the exact question someone asked.

  • Real numbers — prices, timings, success rates
  • A named doctor or expert behind every claim

Case study: client website
audit findings.

A fertility clinic listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand — 2,000+ patients a year, a 60–65% ICSI success rate, three branches, seven doctors. It appeared in no AI answer on any engine. The audit found why.

Most site owners have no idea their site is telling AI agents to stay out.
  • Zero AI answers on any engine
  • English pages serving Thai body copy
  • Core Web Vitals failing at 21/100
Diagnosis
Eight AI crawlers disallowed at robots.txt — a platform default, not a decision.
Consequence
Five clinics recommended. The listed clinic is not among them.
